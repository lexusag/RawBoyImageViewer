The first release of RawBoy Viewer: a mouse-first photo viewer and culling tool for macOS 15+, from the RawBoy family.

- Browser: folders, favourites, a thumbnail grid from the cameras' own previews, sort by shoot date, ratings bar and
  filters; cloud placeholders aren't downloaded by browsing; the folder updates itself when other apps change it.
- Viewer: the photo fills the screen; the edges show actions (left), details with histogram and EXIF (right) and the
  filmstrip (top); the bottom of the photo goes to the previous / next photo.
- Culling: stars, pick, reject in XMP sidecars RawBoy, Lightroom, Capture One and darktable read; `_bin`, Trash, undo.
- Rotate, crop (kept the way RawBoy keeps it), export, compare 2–4 photos, slideshow, real RAW preview.

**Not notarized** (signed with a free Personal Team): the first time, right-click the app › Open, or System Settings ›
Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. Later versions arrive through Check for Updates.
